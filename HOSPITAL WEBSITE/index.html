FREQUENTLY ASKED

Quick answers

How do I know when my doctor is available?

See the OPD Schedule section. Choose a day to view who sits and their timings, or check the weekly timetable.

Can I book for any date?

You can book from today onwards, on days when the selected doctor sits. Booked-out time slots are shown as unavailable.

Is the appointment confirmed instantly?

In this demo the confirmation shows on screen and is saved on your device only. A live hospital site must connect this form to a secure booking system.

What if I need emergency care?

Do not wait for an appointment. Call the emergency helpdesk or an ambulance (108) and go to the nearest emergency department.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Cancel from “Your appointments” under the booking form and book a new slot, or call the helpdesk.
